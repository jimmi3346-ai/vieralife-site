Tru Inspect

Florida inspections, captured on your iPhone or iPad and rendered onto the official forms.

Florida inspectors spend their day on a ladder, in an attic, or under a house — and then spend their evening retyping what they found into a form. Tru Inspect closes that gap. Inspections are captured on the device in your hand — iPhone or iPad — at the property, and rendered straight onto the official state and carrier forms, with photographs attached, ready to deliver before the inspector leaves the driveway.

It covers the five inspections that make up most Florida residential work:

Wind Mitigation

The OIR-B1-1802 uniform mitigation verification form, with roof covering, roof deck attachment, roof-to-wall connection, roof geometry, secondary water resistance, and opening protection captured as they are observed.

Four-Point Inspection

Roof, electrical, plumbing, and HVAC condition and age, in the format carriers and Citizens expect for older homes at binding or renewal.

Roof Certification

Remaining useful life, covering type, and condition, on the certification form carriers accept.

WDO / Termite

The Florida wood-destroying organism inspection report, DACS-13645, including findings, conducive conditions, and the required diagram.

Full Home Inspection

A complete inspection against the Florida standards of practice — structure, roof, exterior, interior, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, and more — with a narrative report built from an editable comment library.

How it works

  • Everything works offline. Inspections, photographs, and signatures are captured on the device. No signal is required at the property.
  • Your data stays yours. Inspections sync through the inspector's own iCloud account. Viera Life operates no server and never receives client records.
  • Reports render on the device. Each inspection produces a PDF the inspector can deliver by email, AirDrop, or Files.

Who we are

Viera Life LLC is a Florida limited liability company, owned and operated by James Hemelgarn, a working licensed home inspector in Brevard County. Tru Inspect is built by the person who uses it — every form, field, and default in the app comes out of real inspections in Florida houses.
